# Code of conduct

Project participants must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on the
work, avoid harassment or discrimination, and protect private or sensitive
information encountered during collaboration.

Unacceptable conduct may be reported privately to the repository owner through
GitHub. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or take other proportionate action to protect the project and its participants.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other
spaces where someone represents the project.
